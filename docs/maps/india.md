## Rules

| Rule | India |
|---|---|
| Players | 2-4 |
| Trains each | 45 |
| Opening hand | 4 cards |
| Opening tickets | 4, keep 2+ |
| Ticket draw | 3, keep 1+ |
| Unkept opening tickets | under the deck |
| Returned tickets | under the deck |
| Double routes | one side only with 2-3 players |
| Locomotives | wild on any route |
| Face-up locomotive | whole turn |
| Face-up row redealt at | 3 locomotives |
| Longest path bonus | 10 |
| Most tickets bonus | none |
| Mandala bonus | see below |
| Rules engine version | 0 |

**Mandala bonus.** A completed ticket counts if its two cities are joined by two of the player's paths that share no
route (they may share cities). The bonus depends on how many tickets count:

| Tickets | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6+ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Bonus | 5 | 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
